# Excluded from this kit

This kit is a sanitised, generic extract of a private working setup. The following categories were deliberately left out. Add your own equivalents as you need them.

* Personal and organisational details: names of people, organisations, projects and communities, contact lists, correspondence, and any document written for a specific client or employer.
* Private applications and their rules, tests, evidence and lessons.
* Smart-home, vehicle and device integrations, network details, and anything that identifies a location or a household.
* Credentials and secrets of every kind: tokens, keys, certificates, local configuration files, saved sign-ins, and local settings overrides.
* Scheduled tasks and automations, including their names, prompts and timings.
* Chat and session logs, run logs, rolling diagnostic logs and handoff notes from real work.
* Film dialogue, scene tables and source claims about fictional characters. The personas here are original, generic styles; the names are nicknames only.
* Paid-service setups, billing details and usage figures.
* Third-party packages, runtimes, models and downloaded reference documents.
* Files for other domains in the private workspace (document templates, logos, writing-comparison skills).
* Personal ethics rulings and the worked examples behind them.
* Incident records, gate logs and audit-register contents from real use.
* Connector identifiers and the exact tool-name lists of a real workspace.
* The private research bay's code, its tool pins and its allowlists, and the page-inspection bay and its launcher.
* The Gemini reader script and any key handling.
* The launcher that calls the Codex assistant, and the identifiers of any chat it uses.
